## Major Clans (Ru) and Sub-Clans (Shingza)

### 1. Minyagpa (མི་ཉག་པ)

**Alternate Names:** Minyakpa, Minyag

**Region:** Widely found in Khumbu and Solu regions

**Historical Note:** One of the oldest recognized Sherpa clans with deep roots in the Khumbu valley.

**Sub-Clans:**
1. **Thaktok** - Traditional sub-clan
2. **Serwa** - Common in middle Khumbu
3. **Khyungba** - Associated with high-altitude settlements
4. **Gole** - Found across multiple regions
5. **Chuserwa** - Less common but well-documented

---

### 2. Thimmi (ཐིམ་མི)

**Alternate Names:** Thimi

**Region:** Common in upper Solu and Rolwaling regions

**Historical Note:** Strong presence in Rolwaling valley and upper Solu-Khumbu areas.

**Sub-Clans:**
1. **Lama** - Note: Can overlap with religious title usage
2. **Chhumbi** - Connected to Chumbi Valley region
3. **Nakchung** - Traditional sub-clan
4. **Phurtewa** - Less common variant

---

### 3. Chakpa (ཆགས་པ)

**Alternate Names:** Chagpa

**Region:** Various settlements, historically linked to trade routes

**Historical Note:** Traditionally associated with trade and early Sherpa settlements along commercial routes.

**Sub-Clans:**
1. **Yulba** - Regional variant
2. **Khangba** - Traditional sub-clan
3. **Serthok** - Less common

---

### 4. Pinasa (པི་ན་ས)

**Alternate Names:** Piniasa

**Region:** Less numerous but recognized across multiple regions

**Historical Note:** Smaller clan with presence in various Sherpa settlements.

**Sub-Clans:**
1. **Paldor** - Named after sacred mountain region
2. **Nima** - Note: Also a common given name
3. **Angba** - Traditional variant

---

### 5. Salaka (ས་ལ་ཀ)

**Alternate Names:** Salakpa

**Region:** Multiple regions

**Historical Note:** Unique in that many sub-clans overlap with common Sherpa given names.

**Sub-Clans:**
1. **Dorge** - Note: Also common as given name (Dorje)
2. **Tenzing** - Note: Famous given name, also sub-clan designation
3. **Lhakpa** - Note: Common given name and sub-clan

## Understanding the Data

### Naming Conventions

**Major Clan (Ru):**
- Primary ancestral lineage
- Indicates deep historical family connection
- Marriage within the same Ru is traditionally prohibited

**Sub-Clan (Shingza):**
- Branch within a major clan
- Indicates extended family grouping
- Marriage within the same Shingza is traditionally prohibited
- Even across different major clans, same Shingza indicates relationship

### Spelling Variations

Many clan names have multiple accepted spellings due to:
- Transliteration from Tibetan script
- Regional pronunciation differences
- Historical documentation variations

**Examples:**
- Minyagpa / Minyakpa / Minyag
- Chakpa / Chagpa
- Pinasa / Piniasa

### Regional Variations

Clan structures and recognition may vary between:
- Khumbu region
- Solu region
- Rolwaling valley
- Diaspora communities
- Different Sherpa settlements

## Marriage Rules

### Traditional Prohibitions

❌ **NOT Allowed:**
1. Same Major Clan (Ru) → Blood relatives
2. Same Sub-Clan (Shingza) → Bone relatives (even if different major clans)

✅ **Allowed:**
- Different Major Clan AND Different Sub-Clan

### Important Considerations

1. **Elder Consultation**: Always mandatory before marriage decisions
2. **Regional Customs**: May have additional considerations
3. **Historical Records**: Family oral histories may reveal unknown connections
4. **Adoptions**: May affect clan relationships
5. **Exceptions**: Rare exceptions may exist in specific circumstances (elder guidance required)
